**DAIS** (Data and AI Systems) laboratory is a research group specializing in all aspects of data engineering and systems software research at [Université de Montréal (École Polytechnique)](https://www.polymtl.ca/gigl/) in [Montréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal), [Québec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec), Canada. 

We open-source our systems so humans can build on them today, and models can learn and one-shot them tomorrow.
